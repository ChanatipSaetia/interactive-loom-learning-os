# Choose components by learning purpose

Ask what each candidate introduces, lets the learner explore, can demonstrate through a response, and cannot establish. Follow only relevant component links; each focused reference includes its exact contracts and required dependencies.

## Explain and orient

Connected explanation, motivation and readable examples.

[TextContent](../components/TextContent.md), [Text](../components/Text.md), [Intro](../components/Intro.md), [CodeBlock](../components/CodeBlock.md), [Callout](../components/Callout.md)

## Compare and evaluate

Compare shared criteria, examples, strengths, limitations and consequences; a decision is optional.

[Table](../components/Table.md), [TradeoffSandbox](../components/TradeoffSandbox.md), [Card](../components/Card.md), [Stack](../components/Stack.md), [Tabs](../components/Tabs.md)

## Follow steps and processes

Execute instructions, understand step connections or inspect a person’s experience.

[Steps](../components/Steps.md), [Bullets](../components/Bullets.md), [Flowchart](../components/Flowchart.md), [UserJourney](../components/UserJourney.md)

## Explore relationships and categories

[ConceptMap](../components/ConceptMap.md), [TaxonomyBrowser](../components/TaxonomyBrowser.md), [PillarLayer](../components/PillarLayer.md), [Svg](../components/Svg.md)
