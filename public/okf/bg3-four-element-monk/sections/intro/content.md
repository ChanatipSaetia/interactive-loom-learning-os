The Four Element Monk is one of the most versatile builds in Baldur's Gate 3. By combining Monk martial arts with elemental spellcasting from four schools — Fire, Cold, Lightning, and Acid — you create a character that excels at both melee combat and ranged magic.

At its core, the build revolves around Ki points, the monk's resource pool. Every elemental ability costs Ki: Twin Elemental Wrath infuses your unarmed strikes for 1 Ki, Four Point Attack unleashes four guaranteed elemental strikes for 4 Ki, and elemental spells spend Ki based on their level.

The Strix Cove Monk Cloak from Act 3 is essential, as it lets you cast spells outside of combat. Without it, your monk spells only work during encounters.

Multiclassing into Sorcerer for 2-4 levels unlocks Metamagic, with Twinned Spell being the most transformative — it lets single-target spells like Ice Knife hit two enemies simultaneously.
