The Poison + Bleed Witchhunter is a dual-ailment build in Path of Exile 2 patch 0.5 that melts enemies through simultaneous Poison and Bleed damage-over-time effects. It combines the Mercenary's Witchhunter ascendancy with two cheap support gems to create a build that scales from budget league starter to demanding endgame maps.

The core loop is simple: hit enemies with attacks that apply both Poison (Chaos DoT) and Bleed (Physical DoT), then step back and watch them tick concurrently. The synergy multipliers are what make this exceptional — Poison 3 increases Poison magnitude against bleeding enemies, Bleed 3 does the reverse, and At Mixture adds 20% more of each magnitude when the target has the other ailment.

The Witchhunter ascendancy amplifies this further. No Mercy grants up to 40% more damage as enemies lose Concentration from your attacks. Obsessive Rituals trades 50% less Armour and Evasion for raw offensive power, making positioning essential. Sorcery Ward offers a unique barrier mechanic that absorbs damage on skill use.

Two skills carry the build: Gas Grenade creates persistent poison clouds on impact, and Barrage fires rapid projectiles that apply both ailments through attack speed. Together they cover screen-wide areas while keeping DoTs fresh across packs.

The build's strongest appeal is its accessibility — Gas Grenade, Barrage, and At Mixture are all inexpensive to roll, and the ascendancy path is compact. But the dual-ailment ceiling is high enough that incremental gear upgrades push into late-league viability.
